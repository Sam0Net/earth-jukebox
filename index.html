How Does Our Planet Sound?

NASA satellites monitor Earth day and night, but maps are not the only way to experience it. This platform translates space imagery into interactive music so everyone (including blind and low-vision individuals) can hear the living rhythms of our planet.

2,200 M People worldwide with vision difficulties. This app makes space science heard, not just seen.
Live Music No pre-recorded loops: every point on the map generates unique notes and rhythms based on real satellite events.
100% Accessible Designed for touch screens, mouse, keyboard navigation, or screen readers with guided assistance.

Wildfires & Heat

Energetic, fast-paced rhythms. As heat and wildfire intensity rise in a region, the music sounds more vibrant and urgent.

Forests & Wetlands

Warm melodic tones and tropical percussion representing living vegetation, wetlands, and natural gas cycles.

Oceans & Currents

Deep, calming soundscapes. The music flows smoothly to mimic oceanic currents and sea surface temperatures.

Spatial Stereo Audio

With headphones, events across the Americas play in your left ear and Asia or Oceania in your right ear to help you locate them on the map.
